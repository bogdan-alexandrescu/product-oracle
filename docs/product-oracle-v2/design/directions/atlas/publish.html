A pet owner booking a first appointment online pays a refundable deposit before the slot is held. Regular clients skip the deposit unless the clinic turns it on for them.

  1. FR-1The booking widget asks for a deposit when the clinic has deposits on and the client is new.
  2. FR-2No-shows cost a typical clinic about 9% of booked slots, so the deposit defaults to £15.

    Evidence for this claim Medium 63%

    • 9.1% of slotsSep 2026Booking export, 58 clinics (upload)

    Disagrees: Sales call notes estimate 4%; older (Mar 2026) and from 2 clinics.

    In Sources and data points › Functional requirements › FR-2

  3. FR-3A deposit is refunded automatically when the owner cancels at least 24 hours before the slot.
  4. FR-4Front-desk staff can waive or refund a deposit from the appointment, with a reason.
