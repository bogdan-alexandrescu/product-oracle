Problem

problem · brief · #9f3a1c2e

Buyers learn the shipping cost only on the final step. In synthetic interviews, most who abandoned did so when shipping appearedUpload1, and the support channel shows the same patternSlack2.

Returning buyers are less affected because their address is saved, so the surprise is concentrated in first orders.
