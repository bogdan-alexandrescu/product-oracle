# Screen 5 · Quality evaluation for a PRD version (nav "prds")

## A · Criteria table + inspector (`5-quality-a.html`, viewing as approver)
- Idea: one row for each readiness item (10 advisory and 3 hard: security_policy, third_party_support, critical_blockers), with Rules · Model · People · Agreement columns. The 380 px inspector shows the selected criterion: its question, the rationale from rules, the model and each rater, its evidence/section tags, its owner, and the rating form (status, rationale, section_keys).
- Trade-off: the most information on one screen and closest to PublishView's readiness panel. Rating happens one criterion at a time, so a person sees the rules and model verdicts before rating (anchoring). The report history is not shown.
- Shows: counts, blocked, policy, evaluated_at, composition_revision, items[].status/rationale/evidence_ids/owner_id/section_keys/error, model (null shows "Skipped"), human[] (latest per rater), agreement.model/human, model_review.disclaimer/model/created_at, latest_report. Header actions: POST evaluations and POST quality-review. Board: job queued/running/failed (pool_paused + next_reset_at, "judged no known criterion"), succeeded output.counts/tokens/cost, 409 switched off, 422 errors, nobody rated, check error, blocked by a hard check, loading, plus viewer, commenter and owner.

## B · Report timeline + compare (`5-quality-b.html`, viewing as owner)
- Idea: GET /v1/evaluation-reports as a dated timeline (rules / model / human evaluators). Selecting a report lists its evaluation-findings next to a second report chosen with "Compare". The inspector shows the selected report's metadata and, for a model report, the LLM job (status, model, tokens, cost, attempts, times).
- Trade-off: the best fit for audit and history ("what did the model say on Tuesday, and what did Ines say?") and the best place for job retry/cancel. The current readiness state of each criterion is secondary, and rating has to happen on another surface.
- Shows: report evaluator/status/summary.counts/blocked/blocking/errors/created_by/created_at, findings criterion/status/rationale/section_keys, the model_output label and disclaimer, LlmJobOut fields. Board: empty reports list, pending/running, queued no_account_available, failed + Retry, no_accounts_registered, 409, rules errors, blocked, a filter with no results, and the role variants.

## C · Guided "rate this version" (`5-quality-c.html`, viewing as commenter)
- Idea: a step-through form with the 10 advisory criteria as cards (question + "reads"). The rules, model and other people's verdicts open only after you choose a status, so they do not anchor the rating. The inspector holds your draft: rated count, agreement so far, the note, and "Submit N ratings" (one POST evaluations/human). Hard checks are listed read-only.
- Trade-off: the cleanest human ratings and the clearest commenter flow. It is weak as an overview for approvers, and the model job controls are pushed to the board/header.
- Shows: CriterionInfo key/title/question/reads, ratings[] {criterion,status,rationale,section_keys} + note, item.model null ("did not judge"), human[] / agreement.human null, item.error, 422 "rate each criterion once" / "unknown criteria: …", the success report id, blocked, 409, viewer read-only, and approver extras.

## Recommendation
A as the main Quality screen. It matches PublishView's readiness vocabulary, shows the dashboard and the three opinions side by side, and keeps rating close to the evidence. Take two things from the others: C's "hide rules/model until you pick a status" as a toggle in A's rating form, and B's report list as a "Reports" tab or inspector section for history and job retry/cancel.

No shared-class requests.
