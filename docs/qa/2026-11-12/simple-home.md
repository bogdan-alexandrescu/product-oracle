# Visual QA: Simple Home, Inbox, Decisions, Meetings (main e5431e8, mock build)

How I ran it: I built the mock app (`npm run build:mock`) and ran it with `start:mock` on port 3393. Crawl specs in `$TMPDIR/qa` (rest/act/meet/probe) use the repo's `e2e/test.ts` clock and `login()`. Each state was run in light and dark, at 1440x900 and 390x844. That gave 235 screenshots in `shots/`, named `<state>-<desktop|phone>-<light|dark>.png`. `steps.log` lists every state, including the few my selectors missed.

Automated checks on every shot:
- **Console errors and page errors: none** (no `console-errors.log` was written).
- **Sideways scroll on the page: none**, at either width. `overflow.log` only lists the Roadmap's own sideways-scrolling timeline, which scrolls on purpose.
- Every count I tried leads to its items: Home's tiles go to `#waiting-guided-setup` and `#broken-links`, "34 in all" goes to Decisions, "6 more" goes to Decisions filtered to the PRD, and "8 more in your Inbox" has Open.

Severity: **blocker** = an action cannot be done or a page breaks; **major** = breaks a rule in oracle-product.md or overlaps/hides content; **minor** = polish or consistency.
