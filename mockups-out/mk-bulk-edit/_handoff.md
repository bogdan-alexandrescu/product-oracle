# mk-bulk-edit: handoff

## What the product has today (read in the mock app, Nov 24)

- **Priorities, ranked list (Simple)**: one row per feature: drag handle (owners and approvers;
  a commenter's drag becomes a proposal, its tip says so), rank, line dot, name (click opens the
  one feature panel), code, Current-state pill (NEW / IN PROGRESS / EXISTS), goal, value,
  confidence, effort, score, ⋯ menu. "Show" filter chips by state. No owner on the row.
- **Roadmap, Now / Next / Later (Simple)**: cards in track bands per column. A card drags to
  another column (arrow keys too) and click opens the panel. A picked card has its own
  `data-picked` look while it drags.
- **No bulk anything**: no multi-select, no bulk status, owner, date or track change, and no bulk
  Undo anywhere.
- **The checkboxes in `web/app/plan/_tracks.tsx` (722, 768)**: they sit in the Detailed track
  form, under "Verified by" (the track's sources) and "Items" (which roadmap items a track
  holds). They are not row selection, so no proposal reuses them.
- **Status words**: the product's own pills are New, In progress and Exists. The bulk status
  uses these three words.
- **Rights**: rows carry `can_reorder` and `applies`. Edits are direct for owners and approvers
  and become proposals for commenters, decided by the approver.

## What the mock-ups do

- All variants are in the real web code, behind `// MOCK-HOOK`:
  - `web/app/simple/priorities/_bulk-mock.tsx`, the whole file.
  - The hook lines in `priorities.tsx`.
  - `web/app/simple/roadmap/_bulk-board-mock.tsx` plus the hook lines in `_board.tsx`.
  - The invented data in `web/lib/api/mock/bulk-mock.ts`.
- URL switches:
  - Decisions: `?be1=a|b|c`, `?be2=a|b|c`, `?be3=a|b|c`.
  - State: `&pick=2,4,6,7` (ranks), `&open=status`, `&set=status:Exists`, `&role=commenter`, `&long=1`, `&mode=1`.
- Frames: `tools/snap.spec.ts` drives the built mock app (`npm run build:mock`, then `vite preview --port 3393`) from `tools/spec.py`'s list. It runs under `swarm-test-browsers npx playwright test -c .mk-snap/snap.config.ts`, with Chromium scrollbars shown, at 1440x900, 1512x982 and 390x844. Each frame is a static html (styles inlined, scripts removed except the `?theme=` hook, fonts in `assets/`) plus a PNG.
- `picks.json` is generated by `tools/picks.py`.

## Not covered

- Detailed: only BE1 A is drawn there (the plan table, `web/app/plan/_table.tsx` MOCK-HOOK lines); BE2 and BE3 reuse the same bar.
- Goal buckets, Inbox and the Timeline/Gantt: not drawn. BE1 A is drawn on Priorities and on the Now / Next / Later board.
- The bulk bar on the board is drawn but not wired (frames stop at "picked").
