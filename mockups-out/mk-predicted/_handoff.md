# mk-predicted: what the pages have today

- Goal map (Simple): goal cards show the measure's progress bar and an At risk / On track pill (about the measure, not dates). No due or predicted date on the card.
- Goal map strip: each milestone shows its code, name and due date. No slip.
- Roadmap (Simple): goal cards show item counts; milestone chips carry a check or warning from the milestone's status; a milestone card opens from its chip with Name, Kind, Status, Date, Track, Measure.
- Feature panel: Dates with their source ("Estimated by Oracle", "Dates from engineering"), Confidence (the feature's effect on the goal), Goal, its Date, Milestone.
- Nothing today shows a predicted date for a goal or a milestone. Detailed mode is unchanged by every proposal.
